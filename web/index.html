How it works

One pipeline. No backtesting IDE, no signal-selling, no noise.

  1. 1

    Define a pattern

    Describe a cross-stock relationship in a graphical canvas — a pair, a lead-lag chain, a ripple, a drift. The pattern is pre-registered before anything is measured.

  2. 2

    The gauntlet runs server-side

    Your candidate goes through staged, pre-registered tests: calibration, discovery screen, permutation nulls, multiplicity correction against the full trial ledger, and walk-forward validation. All mining runs on SIFT's servers.

  3. 3

    Automatic full-depth validation

    Every survivor is re-tested automatically at full universe and full history. This stage is never opt-in and never skipped — coverage is complete by construction.

  4. 4

    Save, monitor, decide yourself

    Surviving patterns become saved connections with live signal matching and decay monitoring. SIFT tells you what survived everything tried — what you do with it is yours.

The public trial registry

Every trial — pass or fail — is recorded in a global, public, scope-stratified ledger. A mining run that finds nothing is a successful run: the kill count is the point.

Survival facts only

Public discovery cards show what a pattern survived — permutation counts, control passes, scope — and nothing else. Never returns, never win rates, never directional calls.

Scope-stratified denominators

A 5-year-window trial and a full-history trial are different tests and never share a denominator. Every scope limit is labeled, never hidden.

Cards can visibly die

When full-depth validation resolves, a public card updates — promoted, or killed. A card that can die in public is the most honest artifact we can publish.

What SIFT never does

  • Trade. No execution, no order routing, no brokerage — for you or for us. Signals, never execution.
  • Serve raw data. No market data reaches any user device in any form. Your browser receives derived outputs only: candidates, scores, stage transitions, signal events.
  • Sell hype. No win rates, no P&L leaderboards, no profit testimonials, no picks, no “hidden alpha” lottery framing. In any SIFT-published content, a ticker never appears next to a directional call.
  • Let validation be optional. Registry-depth validation runs for every survivor, automatically — never opt-in, never popularity-gated.
